I am a master's student (2024–2027) at the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China. My research interests include LLM Alignment & RLHF, LLM Agentic Training. I have published 5 papers as the first/co-first author in NeruIPS, ACL, and ICLR.
